# Device verification

Record the phone model, Android version, RAM, free storage, GPU, and installed model version/hash with results. The project is build-verified separately from this hardware acceptance list.

- Install the APK, open every screen, rotate the device, enlarge system font size, and enable TalkBack. Verify controls remain usable and nothing overlaps.
- Complete an English and Polish voice download. Cancel a download, retry it, and confirm incomplete packages never become available. Delete/reinstall a voice. Check low-storage and disconnected-network errors.
- Use the system camera, cancel it, import a photo, rotate it, and crop around one question. Confirm portrait/landscape EXIF photos appear upright and original gallery files are untouched.
- Solve a known worksheet with printed text, fractions, exponents, diagrams, and several question numbers. Compare transcription and answers against a trusted solution. Try blurry and incomplete photos; check that missing information is flagged.
- Run a full local scan in airplane mode after downloads. Measure cold-start time, solving time, TTS time, peak memory, and temperature over repeated runs. Confirm no network fallback occurs. Test supported GPU models on at least two hardware vendors.
- Connect OpenRouter using an invalid key, a valid key, an account without credits, and unavailable network. Check that only image-capable models are offered. Confirm errors never show the key. Verify API-key deletion.
- Confirm audio starts paused and the ready chime plays once when the player is ready. Disable the chime and verify silence. Compare answers-only and explanation modes.
- Tap once: only rewind. Twice: only forward. Three times: only play/pause. Try slow taps, exact timing boundaries, four taps, dragging, and a long hold following a tap. Adjust the gap and repeat.
- Seek near the beginning/end; verify clamping and that paused playback stays paused. Triple tap after the end restarts. Compare skips against audio timestamps.
- Hold for 1.5 seconds: audio restarts and plays. Release before 3 seconds and confirm the session stays active. Keep holding for 3 seconds: playback stops, home returns, and the app's imported photo, answers, and recording are cleared. Confirm the original gallery photo remains. Test a cancelled/dragged hold and the equivalent TalkBack actions.
- Set system timeout to 15 seconds. Leave listening mode paused for at least 60 seconds and confirm the screen stays on. Exit and verify normal timeout and brightness return. Press the power button and confirm the OS can still lock normally.
- Receive a call, trigger audio focus loss, unplug wired headphones, disconnect Bluetooth headphones, and background the app. Playback should pause and retain position. Returning must not autoplay.
- Pair Redmi Buds 8 Active, prepare a recording, and double-tap to play/pause. Triple-tap to seek forward five seconds. If Xiaomi Earbuds offers previous-track assignment, configure it and confirm a five-second rewind. Test while paused and near recording boundaries. End the session and confirm earbud commands cannot restart the cleared recording. Left/right identity and assistant holds are controlled by earbud firmware.
- Test earbud play from the initial paused recording before any speech has played, with the ready chime disabled, and after another music app was the last player. Check that the listening screen shows the received **Earbuds:** command. If no command appears, try starting/pausing once with the screen triple tap, then retry; also verify gestures and their assignments in Xiaomi Earbuds and a music app.
- Cancel solving and TTS generation, then retry. Repeated cancellation must not leave a native engine running or crash the next request. Try a device without a supported GPU and verify a recoverable error.
- Upload two pages with a question split across the boundary, including a continuation without a repeated question number. Check the model joins the complete question, solves it once, and uses its starting page number. Repeat with a continued table, passage, and answer choices. Compare against trusted answers using each intended model.
- Close/reopen the app after process death: verify ordered photos, selected page, complete transcription, answers, recording, and playback position restore, with audio paused. Repeat after an answer is ready but speech generation fails. End the session, force-stop/reopen, and confirm none of the session returns. Confirm no API key or photo is present in Android backups.
